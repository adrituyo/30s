# The 17 rules, in one line each

The build cites these by number. The full version, with the reason, the check and the fix for each, is in the
`portfolio-audit` skill.

| # | Rule | Where it lives in the content file |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Say what you do, in one sentence | `person.headline` |
| 2 | Show work on the first screen | the first project's first picture; a short headline and proof |
| 3 | Signal level and proof | `person.proof`, `person.role` |
| 4 | Strongest, most relevant work first | the order of `projects` |
| 5 | Few projects, each one edited | how many `projects`, and what you leave out |
| 6 | Readable at a glance | each project's `title`, `summary`, `tags` |
| 7 | Say what your part was | `role`, `team`, `client` or `relationship`; `authorship` |
| 8 | Show one decision | `decision`, or a `note` row |
| 9 | Show the change | `result`, or a before/after row |
| 10 | One clear ask, one way to reach you | `ask`, `person.email` |
| 11 | Aimed at something | the target; everything else follows from it |
| 12 | Works on a phone | built in |
| 13 | Loads fast | image weight and format |
| 14 | Shares well | `site.share_image`, `site.title`, `site.description` |
| 15 | Readable by machines | `site.url`, `facts`, `alt` on pictures |
| 16 | No friction | built in: no loader, no hover-only text; do not leave slots or placeholder text |
| 17 | Who you are and how to reach you, always within reach | built in: the dock, from `person.name`, `person.role`, `person.email` |
